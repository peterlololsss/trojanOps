## Pitch night 2025

- Venue: Ronald Tutor Campus Center, one large room with a projector.
- Attendance: 80 students; 9 teams pitched.
- What worked: a printed schedule at the door and a strict timer.
- What to change: start check-in 30 minutes earlier; the line ran into the first pitch.

## Spring kickoff 2026

- Venue: a lecture room booked through the campus room system.
- Attendance: 45 students.
- What worked: forming project teams in the room instead of by form afterwards.

## Booking notes

- Book rooms at least four weeks ahead; evening slots go first.
- Ask for the room 30 minutes before doors for setup.
- Catering needs a named officer on site to receive the order.
