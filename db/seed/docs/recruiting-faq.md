Startup Club recruits every fall and spring. Anyone can attend the info session; no experience is needed.

## Who can join

Any student, from any major and any year. You do not need a startup idea or a team.

## How recruiting works

1. Come to an info session in the first three weeks of the semester.
2. Fill in the short interest form handed out at the session.
3. Join a project team at the kickoff meeting the following week.

## What members do

- Weekly meetings with a short talk and working time for project teams.
- Pitch night once a semester, where teams present to other students.
- Workshops on interviewing users, building a prototype and presenting.

## Cost

Membership is free. Events are free for members.
