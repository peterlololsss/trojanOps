Each club may receive up to $8,000 in USG funding per year. This is demo text written for TrojanOps, not real USG policy.

## Limits

- Annual cap: up to $8,000 per club per year.
- Funded events: 4 per semester.
- Vendor lead time: approved vendors need 6 instructional weeks of lead time.
- Deadline: requests are due Wednesday at 11:59 PM PT.

## How a request is checked

1. The number of funded events the club has already held this semester.
2. The amount requested, added to what the club has received this year, against the annual cap.
3. The event date against the vendor lead time, counted in instructional weeks.
4. The time the request was submitted against the weekly deadline.

## Notes

- An instructional week is a week in which classes meet.
- A request has to pass every check above to be funded.
