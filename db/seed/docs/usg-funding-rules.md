USC Undergraduate Student Government Funding Department Guidelines, current as of October 21, 2026.

## Section 1: Funding Application Timeline

- Funding applications will be open once a week on Wednesdays and close at 11:59 PM. They will be reviewed on a first come first serve basis until all funds are exhausted. Apply as early as possible to avoid delays caused by school breaks and other technical issues.
- Applications must be submitted at least six academic weeks before the initial payment due date, for approved USC Vendors.
  - The Vendor List serves as a guide of previous vendors the University worked with. Applicants must verify vendor status before submitting their application. Please note, vendor status may change at any time and the list is not exhaustive. We will not accept a vendor not on the list.
  - Travel expenses must be made at least 9 academic weeks prior to the date of initial payment due date. Travel expenses that require this 9 academic week deadline include transportation (airfare or ground) and hotels for an overnight stay.
- Applications received within this six to nine-academic-week deadline will be reviewed by the respective Funding Director. If this deadline is not met the application will be automatically denied.
  - Please be advised that USG Funding does not process applications or provide funding for events during University holidays, breaks, study days, or finals.
  - Review the Funding Application Deadlines to see the event dates that are eligible for funding and the submission deadline for the application.
- All applicants must submit the Post Event Evaluation form found on the USG EngageSC page within three business days of the final day of the event.
  - This Post Event Evaluation form must include pictures of the event with members in attendance.
- Final invoices must be submitted no later than three business days after the final day of the event.
  - Submission of invoices will be the responsibility of the applicant. Failure to do so promptly will result in disciplinary action and potential loss of future funding privileges

## Section 2: Funding Application Overview

- Before applying for funding, make sure to complete the USG Funding Department modules during RSO Recognition. These modules provide valuable information about what the Funding Department funds, showcases past successful events, offers application tips, and provides answers to frequently asked questions. By going through the modules, you can better understand the funding process and increase your chances of a successful application.
- Complete the funding application through EngageSC.
  - A completed application consists of the following:
    - Applicant information
    - Recognized Student Organization information
    - Event Logistics
    - The full budget proposal for the event, including quotes for each individual expense
      - For online purchases, include a screenshot of the cart showing prices, quantities, and total price including all fees.
      - For Amazon purchases, create an Amazon Wish List and provide the link in the budget proposal.
    - Traveler information (if applicable)
      - Including name, DOB, phone number, USC email, USC ID #, and class level of all travelers
      - Point of contact for hotel reservations including an address that matches a photo ID
      - This must be provided within the application to have the request processed
    - Applications requesting funding for travel expenses must include a clear statement of purpose that outlines the reasoning for the travel, including how the trip aligns with the goals/mission of the RSO or individual.
    - Log in information for event registrations if applicable
    - ESRs for on campus events
      - If serving food at event, please request and attach the food and beverage form from Hospitality
    - UEPA Event ID #, if applicable
  - Incomplete applications will be returned to the applicant who must provide the necessary information within 2 business days .
    - Failure to provide the information within 2 business days will result in application denial.
    - 2 business days is equivalent to 48 hours from the notification of an incomplete application
- Once an application is submitted, it will be overseen by the respective Director and Associate Director of one of the four funding boards.
  - The funding boards are:
    - The Professional/Academic Research Fund
    - The Philanthropy Fund
    - The Social and Recreation/Startup Fund
    - The Performing and Visual Arts Fund
- Once the Directors have reviewed the funding applications, they will be further reviewed by Graduate Advisors and USC Campus Activities Professional Staff for verification of the submitted information and to process the application.
- After the application has been processed and confirmed as complete by USC Campus Activities Professional Staff, the Funding Director will promptly update the application status and provide instructions on the next steps required to fulfill the funding request.
- If an application is denied, the applicant will be notified via email from the respective funding board. This email will include a detailed reason for the denial.

## Section 3: Maximum Funding per Academic Year

- Individual organizations may receive a maximum of $8,000 from the USG Funding Department per year.
  - The USG Funding Department will provide funding for up to $4,000 of the total cost associated with on-campus venue rentals and personnel.
  - Events with expenses over $4,000 will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis to ensure effective use of funding.
  - Multi-day events will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
- If a funding board attains a utilization rate of 50% or higher before the conclusion of the Fall semester, the Chief Financial Officer reserves the authority to withdraw said funding board from the application process and order its immediate closure until the beginning of the Spring semester application review process. This action is taken with the intent to ensure that adequate funding remains accessible to apply for during the upcoming Spring semester.

## Section 4: Specific Funding Board Guidelines

- The Professional/Academic Research Fund shall provide funding for events programmed by recognized student organizations that help educate and advance students’ future professional careers and academic research projects conducted by either a USC undergraduate student or a group of USC students, the majority of which are undergraduate students.
  - Examples include, but are not limited to:
    - Programs that actively promote professional development and/or contribute to developing leadership skills will be considered.
      - Retreats, conferences, development activities, etc.
      - If applying for conference funding, submit the credentials for each login to the portal of the conference, similar to how you submit necessary identifying information for flights.
      - Events can be on or off campus if the potential career development can be conveyed to the Professional Fund and must be free and open to all students.
    - Programs and events which contribute to a student’s research interests:
      - Conferences, seminars, workshops, and other academic events at the discretion of the board at which research is a component
      - Certain Research & Fieldwork Supplies (Case-by-Case Basis)
- The Philanthropy Fund shall provide funding for community service projects sponsored by recognized student organizations.
  - Examples include, but are not limited to:
    - Planned programs that focus on hands-on community service
      - No payments can be made for any type of service on behalf of a non-profit for a project.
      - For FMS expenses, use a past invoice or provide an official estimate (more than an Excel sheet)
- The Social and Recreation/Startup Fund shall provide funding for events programmed by recognized student organizations to celebrate, highlight, or foster a sense of community for the student group and student organizations first registered with the Office of Campus Activities within the last two academic semesters (fall or spring) for the use of promotion and membership recruitment
  - Examples include, but are not limited to:
    - Programs that provide students with social opportunities that might otherwise not be possible
      - Conventions, retreats, team building activities, etc.
    - Promotional materials, any other goods that will promote the brand of the organization and attract members (such as t-shirts, banners, etc). T-Shirts funded by USG may only be produced by a vendor who is authorized to work with USC Trademarks.
  - Events on or near campus are preferred.
  - Travel to events off campus can be funded if proven that the event will contribute to the club’s mission and closely correlates to its focus.
- The Performing and Visual Arts Fund shall provide funding for events geared towards the arts programmed by recognized student organizations to celebrate, highlight, or foster a sense of community.
  - Examples include, but are not limited to:
    - Expenses related to production, staging, lighting, decoration (rentals only), venue, staffing, and security can only be funded if they are obtained from a PO Vendor.
    - Printing costs for event materials and promotional needs

## Section 5: General Fund Guidelines

- Food Requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and will be assigned to one of the four funds based on the purpose of the event.
  - Additional information detailing why food is necessary and how it aligns with the event's purpose is required for review and approval.
  - RSOs must provide a Health Permit and Certificate of Insurance from the Food Vendor.
  - Food requests should not exceed a $35 per person maximum based on expected attendance, and raw food items requiring refrigeration or preparation are not eligible.
- Lodging Requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and will be assigned to one of the four funds based on the purpose of the event.
  - Proof of price comparison with other hotels is required for hotel expenses.
    - Choose the most financially reasonable option within your situation, including location, event hotel discounts, etc.
    - Rooms cannot exceed $400 per night.
  - Additional information detailing why lodging is necessary and how it aligns with the event's purpose is required for review and approval.

The following are not eligible for funding:

- Any reimbursements
- Payments or donations to charity
- Alcoholic beverages
- Gifts and gift cards
- Durable goods (Items that are not for one-time use such as electronics, uniforms, etc.)
- Raw food items requiring refrigeration and/or preparation
- Restaurant dinners
- Rideshare costs (Uber, Lyft, Buses, Vans, Amtrak cabins, Car rentals, etc.)
- Any refundable deposits and fees pertaining to cleaning/damage
- Peer to Peer Payments (Airbnb, VRBO, PayPal, Zelle, CashApp, etc.)
- Payments to talent or performers
- Monthly Subscriptions/Payments
- Events that include ticket sales
- On-campus events that are not open to all students
- Events occurring in private residences
- International travel
- Jewelry
- Balloons

Any items critical to the function of the program may be considered as an exception to this rule at the board’s discretion.

## Section 6: University Policy and Application Denials

- These guidelines are dependent on all university activity functioning under normal circumstances. USG follows all restrictions applied to university-sponsored events, which will also apply to all RSOs funded. A violation of these rules may result in a permanent ban from USG funding.
  - Actions deemed a violation of the USC Integrity and Accountability Code, USC Prohibited Discrimination, Harassment, and Retaliation Policy, and/or the USC Student Handbook may result in application denial and/or a permanent ban from funding for the individual who submitted the application.
  - As USG Officers, all members of the funding department will adhere to the above policies and the USG Policies Manual. Violations of these policies may be reported internally via the ORCA process or to Professional Staff.
  - In the case of permanent funding bans, Professional Staff will be consulted and will have final say in whether or not an individual will be permanently banned.
- The Funding department may deny applications for a multitude of reasons, this may include, but is not limited to:
  - Past instances of misuse of funds within the academic year
  - Inappropriate contact with funding department members and/or Professional Staff within the academic year
  - Conflicting events requested on the same day
  - RSO receiving funding from another University income source
  - Low remaining funds due to high utilization

## Section 7: Approval and Amendments

- These Funding Department Guidelines shall be approved annually by a majority vote of the Funding Department and the Senate.
- Amendments to this document shall also be approved by majority vote of the Funding Department and Senate.
