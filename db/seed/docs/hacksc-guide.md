# HackSC community guide

HackSC brings student organizers together to plan a student hackathon. New organizers can join the operations, participant experience, or partnerships team.

## Getting involved

Ask Sam, the president, which team needs help. First-time organizers review the member meeting minutes and the previous president's handoff before planning an event.

Financial records are restricted to the president and treasurer. Members can ask an authorized officer for an approved summary.
