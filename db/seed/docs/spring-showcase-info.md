The spring showcase is on April 18 at Bovard Auditorium. Tickets are free for students.

## Details

- Date: April 18
- Doors: 6:30 PM
- Show: 7:00 PM, about 90 minutes with one intermission
- Venue: Bovard Auditorium

## Tickets

- Free for students with a student ID; reserve a seat at the Dance Club table or at the door.
- Seating is first come, first served.

## Program

- Twelve pieces across hip-hop, contemporary, ballroom and cultural styles.
- Every Dance Club team performs, and the beginner class opens the second half.

## Getting involved

Students who want to help backstage or at the door can ask any Dance Club officer.
