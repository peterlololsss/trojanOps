The archery budget covers range fees and new targets. Range fees are $1,100 for the year.

Prepared by Morgan (treasurer). Demo text.

## Lines

- Range fees: $1,100
- New targets: $450
- Arrows and spare strings: $300
- Safety gear for beginners: $150
- Total: $2,000

## Notes

- Range fees are paid in two halves, one at the start of each semester.
- Targets are replaced once a year, before the fall beginner sessions.
- Equipment bought by the club stays with the club and is signed out at each practice.
