# HackSC president handoff — 2025–2026

Prepared by the 2025–2026 president for the incoming organizing team.

## President's highlighted lesson

“Our biggest mistake was treating a room request as a confirmed reservation, then running one queue for registered participants, walk-ins and help. The reservation process and check-in queue became confused, and the opening briefing was delayed.”

## What each stage needs

Venue reservation needs written approval, a named owner, a capacity check and a backup plan. Registration needs one source of truth and a clear confirmation message. Check-in needs a rehearsal, visible signs, separate lanes and a queue lead who coordinates with the stage lead.

Building sessions need published mentor hours and a staffed help desk. Project submission needs a deadline, a tested upload process and announced judging criteria. Judging needs a timed rotation and a contact for missing teams. Closing needs awards verification, participant follow-up and a retrospective with named owners.

## Recommended new process

Six weeks before, obtain written venue confirmation or escalate to the backup plan. Four weeks before, open a single registration list and confirm volunteers and mentors. Two weeks before, rehearse the three-lane check-in process. On event day, open doors 45 minutes before the briefing and have the queue lead coordinate admission with room capacity. Publish mentor hours, submission rules and judging criteria before the build begins. After awards, assign follow-up owners and hold the retrospective within seven days.

The incoming team should confirm dates and capacity with the venue before using this proposed schedule. Ask the treasurer about authorized finance approvals separately.
