# HackSC organizing meeting — October 8, 2026

Present were Sam (president), Taylor (treasurer), Jordan and Casey (organizing members).

## Previous event process

The previous student hackathon followed this sequence: request a room, recruit mentors and volunteers, open participant registration, check in participants, hold the opening briefing, support building sessions, collect projects, judge demos, and close with awards and a retrospective.

## Problems reviewed

The room request was treated as a confirmed booking before written approval arrived. Registration and check-in used different lists, so volunteers repeatedly rechecked names. A single queue mixed registered participants, walk-ins and help requests. The opening briefing started late while the line was still moving.

## Decisions for the next event

1. Start the room request six weeks before the event. Sam owns written confirmation, capacity, setup access and the escalation contact. Keep a backup room until confirmation arrives.
2. Four weeks before the event, Casey publishes one registration form and maintains the single attendee list. Confirm mentor availability and volunteer shifts at the same checkpoint.
3. Two weeks before the event, Jordan rehearses check-in with volunteers and assigns a queue lead. Use separate registered, walk-in and help lanes, plus visible signs.
4. On event day, open check-in 45 minutes before the briefing. Confirm the attendee list and room capacity before admitting walk-ins. The queue lead tells the stage lead when the briefing can start.
5. During building sessions, post mentor hours, the help desk location and a clear escalation channel. Publish submission rules and judging criteria before participants begin.
6. At closing, verify project submissions, run the judging rotation, announce awards and record follow-up owners. Hold the retrospective within seven days.

## Ownership

Sam confirms the venue. Casey maintains registration and participant communications. Jordan leads check-in and volunteer rehearsal. Taylor handles officer-only finance approval. The member handoff contains operational lessons and excludes financial amounts.
